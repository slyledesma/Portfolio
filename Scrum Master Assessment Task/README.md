## 🛡️ Strategic Context & Assumptions
Before diving into the strategy, it is important to establish the framework of this diagnosis:
* **Evidence-Based:** These conclusions assume a discovery phase was conducted, including team retrospectives, stakeholder interviews, and a workflow audit.
* **Experience-Driven:** Priorities were selected based on **7 years of experience** as an Agile Project Manager, focusing on high-impact areas that stabilize aggressive release windows.
* **Acknowledged Variables:** While this plan focuses on process and alignment, I acknowledge that technical success also depends on factors like **CI/CD health**, **Technical Debt**, and **Team Skill Gaps**, which would be monitored in parallel.

## 🔍 1. Diagnosis & Priorities (The Core Triad)
[cite_start]To eliminate friction before scaling, the strategy focuses on **Clarity, Flow, and Dependencies**[cite: 27, 34].

### A. Clarity – Requirements Definition (DoR / DoD)
* [cite_start]**Problem:** Ambiguous user stories leading to missed objectives[cite: 36].
* [cite_start]**Validation:** Audit the backlog for >30% mid-sprint scope changes or vague acceptance criteria[cite: 37].
* [cite_start]**Action:** Establish a strict **Definition of Ready (DoR)** and a formalized **Definition of Done (DoD)**[cite: 38, 39]. [cite_start]No story enters a sprint without meeting the DoR[cite: 40].

### B. Dependencies – Cross-Team Management
* [cite_start]**Problem:** Constant blockers preventing work completion[cite: 42].
* [cite_start]**Validation:** Track "Blocked" time per task and analyze daily impediments[cite: 43, 44].
* [cite_start]**Action:** Create a **Visual Dependency Map** (AI, Infra, APIs) and commit to resolving cross-team blockers in **<24 hours**[cite: 45, 46].

### C. Flow – Predictability & Velocity
* [cite_start]**Problem:** A persistent gap between commitment and actual delivery[cite: 48].
* [cite_start]**Validation:** Analyze the last 3 Sprint Burndown/Velocity charts and measure the **Say/Do Ratio** (Target >80%)[cite: 49, 50].
* [cite_start]**Action:** Use **Average Velocity** as a hard planning ceiling and control Scope Creep (>10% is flagged as a risk)[cite: 51, 52].

## 🛠️ 2. Execution Plan (Weeks 1–6)
[cite_start]The goal is to "clean the pipeline" to ensure a frictionless workflow[cite: 53].

| Phase | Strategic Focus | Key Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weeks 1-2** | **Refinement** | [cite_start]Intensive Backlog Refinement sessions; translating business needs into clear technical specs and mapping bottlenecks[cite: 54, 55, 57]. |
| **Weeks 3-4** | **Team Shielding** | [cite_start]Restore strict Scrum cadence; plan based on real velocity with a **15–20% technical buffer** for AI/Infra contingencies[cite: 58, 60, 61, 62]. |
| **Weeks 5-6** | **Stabilization** | [cite_start]Introduce Health KPIs (Lead/Cycle Time) for early bottleneck detection and stabilize the **ML testing environment**[cite: 65, 66, 67, 68]. |

## 🚀 3. Release Strategy (10-Week Mark)
[cite_start]Ensuring on-time delivery through strict governance and proactive risk management[cite: 70].

* [cite_start]**Scope Management:** Apply **MoSCoW prioritization** to protect "Must-have" core SaaS functionality[cite: 71, 72].
* [cite_start]**Risk Management (ROAM):** Maintain a proactive Risk Register and classify every threat as **Resolved, Owned, Accepted, or Mitigated**[cite: 73, 74].
* [cite_start]**Governance & Alignment:** * **Weekly Stakeholder Syncs:** Metrics-based reporting to ensure transparency[cite: 75, 76].
    * [cite_start]**Feature Freeze (Week 8):** Zero tolerance for late scope expansion[cite: 77, 79].
    * [cite_start]**Final 2 Weeks:** 100% focus on QA, critical fixes, and deployment readiness[cite: 78].

## 📈 Expected Outcomes
* [cite_start]**Predictability:** Achievement of a **>85% Say/Do Ratio**, creating reliable roadmaps[cite: 81].
* [cite_start]**Operational Speed:** Significant reduction in Lead & Cycle Time via proactive impediment removal[cite: 82].
* [cite_start]**Quality:** A stable release supported by a strict DoD and dedicated stabilization period[cite: 83].
* [cite_start]**Data-Driven Culture:** Transitioning from "feelings" to transparency using real-time KPIs and the **ROAM framework**[cite: 84].
