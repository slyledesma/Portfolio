# Case Study: AI Fleet Monitoring SaaS – Stabilization & Launch Strategy

---

## 📋 The Assignment (Case Study Instructions)
**Role:** Scrum Master joining a team building an AI-powered fleet monitoring SaaS platform.
**Current Context:** The project faces delivery challenges such as missed sprint goals, unclear requirements, and complex cross-team dependencies.
**Objective:** A major release is planned in 10 weeks.
**Key Tasks:** 
* Provide a Diagnosis and define Priorities (Top 3 issues and validation).
* Create an Execution Plan for the next 4–6 weeks to stabilize delivery.
* Define a Release Strategy for the 10-week mark, covering scope, risks, and alignment.

---

## 🛡️ Strategic Context & Assumptions
Before diving into the strategy, it is important to establish the framework of this diagnosis:
**Evidence-Based:** These conclusions assume a discovery phase was conducted, including team retrospectives, stakeholder interviews, and a workflow audit.
**Experience-Driven:** Priorities were selected based on **7 years of experience** as a Scrum Master, focusing on high-impact areas that stabilize aggressive release windows.
**Acknowledged Variables:** While this plan focuses on process and alignment, I acknowledge that technical success also depends on factors like **CI/CD health**, **Technical Debt**, and **Team Skill Gaps**, which would be monitored in parallel.

---

## 🔍 1. Diagnosis & Priorities (The Core Triad)
To eliminate friction before scaling, the strategy focuses on **Clarity, Flow, and Dependencies**.

### A. Clarity – Requirements Definition (DoR / DoD)
* **Problem:** Ambiguous user stories leading to missed objectives.
* **Validation:** Audit the backlog for >30% mid-sprint scope changes or vague acceptance criteria.
* **Action:** Establish a strict **Definition of Ready (DoR)** and a formalized **Definition of Done (DoD)**. No story enters a sprint without meeting the DoR.

### B. Dependencies – Cross-Team Management
* **Problem:** Constant blockers preventing work completion.
* **Validation:** Track "Blocked" time per task and analyze daily impediments.
* **Action:** Create a **Visual Dependency Map** (AI, Infra, APIs) and commit to resolving cross-team blockers in **<24 hours**.

### C. Flow – Predictability & Velocity
* **Problem:** A persistent gap between commitment and actual delivery.
* **Validation:** Analyze the last 3 Sprint Burndown/Velocity charts and measure the **Say/Do Ratio** (Target >80%).
* **Action:** Use **Average Velocity** as a hard planning ceiling and control Scope Creep (>10% is flagged as a risk).

---

## 🛠️ 2. Execution Plan (Weeks 1–6)
The goal is to "clean the pipeline" to ensure a frictionless workflow.

| Phase | Strategic Focus | Key Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weeks 1-2** | **Refinement** | Intensive Backlog Refinement sessions; translating business needs into clear technical specs and mapping bottlenecks. |
| **Weeks 3-4** | **Team Shielding** | Restore strict Scrum cadence; plan based on real velocity with a **15–20% technical buffer** for AI/Infra contingencies. |
| **Weeks 5-6** | **Stabilization** | Introduce Health KPIs (Lead/Cycle Time) for early bottleneck detection and stabilize the **ML testing environment**. |

---

## 🚀 3. Release Strategy (10-Week Mark)
Ensuring on-time delivery through strict governance and proactive risk management.

**Scope Management:** Apply **MoSCoW prioritization** to protect "Must-have" core SaaS functionality.
**Risk Management (ROAM):** Maintain a proactive Risk Register and classify every threat as **Resolved, Owned, Accepted, or Mitigated**.
**Governance & Alignment:** 
* **Weekly Stakeholder Syncs:** Metrics-based reporting to ensure transparency.
* **Feature Freeze (Week 8):** Zero tolerance for late scope expansion.
* **Final 2 Weeks:** 100% focus on QA, critical fixes, and deployment readiness.

---

## 📈 Expected Outcomes
1. **Predictability:** Achievement of a **>85% Say/Do Ratio**, creating reliable roadmaps.
2. **Operational Speed:** Significant reduction in Lead & Cycle Time via proactive impediment removal
3. **Quality:** A stable release supported by a strict DoD and dedicated stabilization period.
4. **Data-Driven Culture:** Transitioning from "feelings" to transparency using real-time KPIs and the **ROAM framework**.
